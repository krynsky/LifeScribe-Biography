# LifeScribe v0.2.2

## New: choose where your data lives

**Settings → Data location** shows where the app keeps your data and lets you move it to a folder of your choice,
for example on another drive.

- The app saves a backup, then copies your database, images, imported photos and backups to the new folder. It
  updates the saved file locations, including the images inside your stories, and switches to the new folder
  when you restart.
- Your previous data is left exactly where it was, as a safety copy. Delete it yourself once you're happy.
- If you pick a folder that already holds LifeScribe data, the app offers to switch to it without copying or
  overwriting anything.
- It warns about folders synced by OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive and similar services, because syncing a
  database while the app is using it can corrupt it.
- If a chosen folder is on a drive that isn't connected at startup, the app says so and explains how to recover.
- Tip: try it first with a new, empty folder. There is no "move back to default" button; to go back, move to your
  old folder or delete `data-location.json` in `%APPDATA%\com.lifescribe.app`.

## Fixed

- The version number at the bottom of the sidebar always said **v0.1.0**. It now shows the real version, and so

## Under the hood

- The location logic lives in `src-tauri/src/data.rs` and `src/lib/data-location.ts`. All images, imported
  photos, backups and thumbnails now resolve through it.
- The database is no longer preloaded at startup, so a chosen folder is respected from the first query.
