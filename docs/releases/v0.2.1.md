# LifeScribe v0.2.1

A polish release for the standalone app: links in stories now work correctly, and the family tree is clearer and
shows everyone.

## Fixed

- **Links in stories no longer open a browser window.** In the editor, a plain click on a link only places the
  cursor. Ctrl/Cmd+click, or the new **Open** button in the link box, follows it. Links to other stories, albums
  and sections open inside the app. In Read mode a plain click follows the link.

## Family tree

- **Clearer layout.** Cards have more room between them and between generations, and the connecting lines are
  thicker, so it's easy to see who belongs to whom.
- **Divorced marriages.** On a family member's page, each marriage has a **Mark as divorced** button. A former
  spouse is drawn on the opposite side with a dashed card and a "Divorced" label, and isn't assumed to be a
  child's other parent.
- **Siblings in birth order.** Children and siblings are drawn oldest first. People without a birth date go last.
- **Full names.** Names wrap onto extra lines instead of being cut off, such as "Alicia Krynsky (née Spika)".
- **Everyone is shown.** Two people who are both a parent of the same child are drawn as a couple, even if no
  marriage was recorded. Anyone outside the centered person's own line, like in-laws, appears under the chart
  in **Also in your family**, with how they're related. Click a card to center the tree on them.

## Upgrading from 0.2.0

The first launch makes one small database change (an "ended" flag on marriages), so existing relationships stay
as they were. Use **Settings → Back up now** first if you like.
