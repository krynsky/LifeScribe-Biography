# LifeScribe v0.2.0 — the standalone release

LifeScribe is now a fully standalone desktop app. It no longer connects to, imports from, or publishes to the
LifeScribe website. Everything you create lives on your computer.

The last version that synced with lifescribe.us is preserved as the tag **`v1-web-connected`** (branch
`archive/web-connected`).

## Highlights

- **No website connection.** The sync engine, publish and conflict screens, account settings and website network
  permissions are gone.
- **Edit and read in one app.** A Read/Edit toggle in the sidebar switches the whole app between writing and a
  finished, read-only view of your biography.
- **Every section opens its own editor page.** Stories, Timeline, Career, Places, Family Tree and Albums are
  edited on full pages instead of pop-up windows. Click anywhere on a card to edit it.
- **Links inside your biography.** A link in a story can point to another story, an album, or a section, and it
  opens inside the app. Web and email links open in your browser. In the link box, use "Or link to" to pick
  something from your biography. While editing, Ctrl/Cmd+click a link (or use the Open button) to follow it.
- **Back up your data.** Settings → Back up now saves a snapshot of your database. The newest 10 are kept.
- **Your name on every story.** The byline comes from your Profile name, so you can change it in one place.

## Also improved

- The whole cover image shows in the picker instead of a cropped strip.
- Places: story and gallery links on a place appear as in the website's design, with a "Stories" and "Galleries"
  list in the place details.
- Place editing has its own map and place search on the same page.
- Family Tree: after adding a person you land on their page, ready to add relationships.
- Images that stories used to load from the website are now stored in the app, so they keep working offline.

## Upgrading from 0.1.0

The first launch updates your database automatically (three small changes: it removes the website sync records,
turns each album's website cover into a chosen cover photo, and moves the story author name into your Profile).
**Take a backup first** by copying `lifescribe.db` from `%APPDATA%\com.lifescribe.app\`. Don't open a database upgraded by 0.2.0 with the archived web-connected version.

## Removed

- Sync with lifescribe.us: Refresh, Review & publish, Reset, conflict resolution, and the connection settings.
- The per-story author name field (replaced by the Profile name).

## Under the hood
