What should the Reader Mode cover page feel like?

This is the first thing you see when entering Reader Mode — before picking a section from the sidebar.

A

Editorial / book cover

Full-bleed hero photo (avatar or a favorite image) behind the title and tagline, large elegant serif typography, like the cover of a memoir. Quiet, emotional, minimal chrome.

My Life Story
A collection of memories, 1978–present
B

Highlights dashboard

Title + tagline up top, then a grid of "highlight" cards below — featured story, recent milestone, a photo, a map preview — each clickable to jump straight into that section. More functional, less ceremonial.

My Life Story
Featured story
Latest photo
Timeline
C

Timeline-first

No separate "cover" — entering Reader Mode drops you straight into the Timeline section (most recent or earliest milestone), title shown small at the top. Reading starts immediately rather than pausing on a title screen.

My Life Story
Timeline entries begin immediately

My recommendation: A — editorial/book cover. A biography app's whole emotional value is "this feels like a real story about my life," and a quiet, photo-forward cover sells that in the first second. B is more useful as a utility dashboard (which the existing Overview page already covers), and C skips the moment entirely.
