How should you navigate between sections inside Reader Mode?

Sections: Overview/Cover, Stories, Timeline, Gallery, Family Tree, Career, Maps

A

Persistent left sidebar (like a book's TOC)

A slim table-of-contents sidebar stays visible on the left the whole time you're in Reader Mode, listing all sections. Click any to jump. Familiar, always-available navigation.

B

Top tab bar

Sections shown as tabs across the top, like chapters of a book laid flat. More horizontal space for content since there's no sidebar; feels more like a document/magazine than an app.

C

Cover page with chapter tiles, then back-and-forth

Landing on Reader Mode shows a cover page with big tappable tiles per section (no persistent nav). Inside a section, small "← Back to Cover" + prev/next chapter arrows are the only navigation — most book-like, but slower to jump between distant sections.

My recommendation: A — persistent left sidebar. It's the least risky choice: familiar, keeps every section one click away, and works well at any window size. C is the most "book-like" but adds friction jumping between sections (e.g. Stories → Maps), and B trades away vertical chrome for not much benefit here.
